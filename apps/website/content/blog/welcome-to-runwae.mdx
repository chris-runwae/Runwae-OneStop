The trips that matter most almost never start with a booking. They start
in the group chat — a shared photo of a beach, a screenshot of a festival
lineup, the words "we should actually go this time."

And then, almost always, they stall. Spreadsheets get out of date. One
person becomes the unpaid travel agent. Someone misses the cut-off on
flights. The trip happens, but a smaller version of it. Or it doesn't
happen at all.

## The gap between intention and experience

We started Runwae to close that gap. Not by adding another tab to your
browser — by replacing every tab you open between "we should go" and
"we're there."

In one app, your group can:

- discover the events worth flying out for
- build the itinerary together, day by day
- book flights, stays and activities as a group
- split the cost without chasing screenshots in iMessage

## Built for friends, creators and communities

Runwae works the same whether it's six friends going to a wedding or six
hundred subscribers going to a creator retreat. The shape of the trip
changes; the tools don't.

If you're an event host, that means a branded portal for your community
and a real revenue stream from the travel they were already going to
book. If you're a travel partner, it means group bookings from people
who are already on the way to your city.
