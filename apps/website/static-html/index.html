Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about planning group travel with Runwae. Still have questions? Email us.

After creating your trip, tap the "Invite" button to generate a shareable link. Send it via WhatsApp, iMessage, or any app. Friends click the link, create a free account (or log in), and they're instantly added to your trip as collaborators. They can view the itinerary, vote on options, and see real-time updates.

Yes! Runwae integrates with major flight and hotel providers so you can search, compare, and book without leaving the app. Your entire group can see available options, vote on their favorites, and complete the booking together. All bookings appear on your shared itinerary automatically.

When anyone pays for a group expense—flights, accommodation, dinner, activities—they log it in Runwae. The app automatically calculates each person's share based on who was involved. You can see a live balance for every member, and request payment or settle up directly within the app. No more awkward money conversations or lost spreadsheets.

Runwae's core features—trip creation, itinerary building, collaboration, and expense splitting—are completely free for travelers. We earn a small commission on bookings made through the app, which keeps the product free for you. Premium features for power users are coming soon.

Runwae is built for any group travel occasion—concerts, music festivals, sports events, bachelor/bachelorette parties, weddings, weekend getaways, cultural events, and friend vacations. Whether it's 4 friends or 40, if you're traveling together, Runwae makes it stress-free.

Every booking made through Runwae—flights, hotels, activities—earns you reward points. Points accumulate in your wallet and can be redeemed for discounts on future bookings. Group organizers earn bonus points for every member they bring on board. No credit card or subscription required to start earning.
