5. Bookings, Payments & Cancellations

5.1 Booking Process

All bookings are subject to availability and provider confirmation. Runwae pre-authorizes but does not capture payment until a provider confirms a booking. In the event a provider cannot confirm, any authorization hold will be released.

5.2 Commissions

Event Hosts earn a commission on travel bookings made by attendees through their Runwae event link. Commissions are tracked against a unique event_id associated with each booking. Commission status follows the following lifecycle:

  • PENDING — booking confirmed but travel date has not yet passed
  • EARNED — travel date has passed and no dispute is open
  • PAID — funds disbursed to the Host’s registered payout account
  • REVERSED — booking was cancelled or refunded, commission voided

Runwae reserves the right to adjust commission rates at its discretion, with at least 14 days written notice to affected Hosts.

5.3 Refunds & Cancellations

Refund eligibility is governed by the applicable provider’s cancellation policy at the time of booking. Runwae will facilitate refund requests but cannot guarantee outcomes where the provider’s policy does not permit a refund. Platform service fees charged by Runwae are non-refundable unless a booking is cancelled due to Runwae’s error.

5.4 Group Payments
