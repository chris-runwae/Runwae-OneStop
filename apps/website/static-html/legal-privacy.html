Runwae, Inc. ("Runwae", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to your data. It applies to all users of the Runwae Platform, including runwae.io and our mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password
  • Profile information: profile photo, travel preferences, nationality
  • Payment information: billing details (processed by Stripe Connect or Paystack — Runwae does not store raw card numbers)
  • Event Host information: event details, payout account details, commission preferences
  • Partner information: business name, registration details, contact information, partner type
  • Communications: messages sent to support, feedback, survey responses

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Behavioral data: pages visited, features used, booking flow interactions, time on platform
  • Location data (where permitted): approximate location to power proximity-based features
  • Cookies and tracking technologies (see Cookies & Tracking section below)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social login providers (e.g., Google, Apple) if you choose to connect
  • Travel API providers (LiteAPI, Duffel, Viator) — booking confirmation and status data
  • Payment processors — transaction confirmation and fraud signals

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information in the following circumstances:

3.1 With Travel Providers

When you make a booking, we share the information necessary to complete that booking with the relevant hotel, flight, or experience provider (e.g., name, contact details, travel dates).

3.2 With Event Hosts

If you book travel through an event link, the Event Host receives aggregated booking data (number of attendees, booking volume) to facilitate commission tracking. Your individual booking details are not shared without your consent.

3.3 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted vendors who process it on our behalf, including payment processors, authentication providers, database infrastructure, and customer support tools. These vendors are bound by data processing agreements.

3.4 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Runwae, our users, or the public.

3.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same protections described here.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Upon account deletion, we will remove or anonymize personal data within 90 days, unless retention is required by law (e.g., tax records, fraud investigations).

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Platform, remember preferences, analyze usage, and personalize experiences. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or our in-app privacy controls. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect Platform functionality.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. Contact us at tech@runwae.io if you believe a minor has submitted data.

8. International Transfers

Runwae operates primarily from the United States. If you are located in the EU, UK, or another jurisdiction with data transfer restrictions, your data may be transferred to the U.S. and other countries. We implement appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required under GDPR.

9. Regulatory Compliance

Runwae is committed to compliance with applicable data protection laws, including:

  • GDPR (EU/UK) — General Data Protection Regulation
  • CCPA (California) — California Consumer Privacy Act
  • NDPR (Nigeria) — Nigeria Data Protection Regulation

Where these laws grant you additional rights, we honor those rights within the applicable scope.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you via email or in-app notification. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
